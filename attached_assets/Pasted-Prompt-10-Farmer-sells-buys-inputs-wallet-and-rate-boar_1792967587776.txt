CONCEPT
The FPC buys produce from the farmer and also sells farm inputs (seed, fertiliser, pesticide) to the same farmer. The farmer has a WALLET at the FPC. Sale proceeds are credited to the wallet, input purchases are debited from the wallet, and the balance stays in the wallet until the farmer withdraws it to the bank. Net rule: wallet balance = total sales credited - total input purchases - withdrawals + other credits (dividend, bonus).
